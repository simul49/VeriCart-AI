# 7. Product Goals
## 7.1 Introduction
The purpose of VeriCart AI is not merely to build another online shopping website.
Its objective is to create an **AI-powered Trust Intelligence Platform** that enables customers to make confident purchasing decisions through transparent Artificial Intelligence.
The product combines modern e-commerce technologies, explainable AI, and software engineering best practices to improve trust, transparency, and user experience.
This chapter defines the strategic goals that guide every aspect of product planning, software development, AI integration, and future expansion.
---
# 7.2 Product Vision Alignment
The goals of VeriCart AI are directly aligned with its vision.
Instead of focusing only on product sales, the platform prioritizes:
- Customer Trust
- Explainable AI
- Intelligent Shopping
- Transparency
- Personalized Experience
Every feature developed throughout the project should contribute to at least one of these objectives.
---
# 7.3 Primary Product Goal
Develop a modern AI-powered e-commerce platform capable of assisting customers throughout the purchasing journey by providing trustworthy, explainable, and intelligent shopping insights.
The platform should successfully combine traditional e-commerce functionality with Artificial Intelligence while maintaining excellent usability, scalability, and security.

# 7.13 Product Success Criteria
VeriCart AI will be considered successful when:

- AI successfully analyzes reviews.
- Trust Scores are generated automatically.
- Customers understand AI recommendations.
- Frontend and backend communicate reliably.
- APIs function correctly.
- Database operations remain consistent.
- Docker deployment succeeds.

# 7.16 Chapter Summary
The goals of VeriCart AI extend beyond building an online shopping website.
The platform seeks to redefine digital commerce by combining Artificial Intelligence, explainable trust analysis, multilingual review understanding, and personalized recommendations within a modern software engineering architecture.
These goals provide the strategic direction for the functional requirements, system design, and implementation plans described throughout the remainder of this Product Requirements Document.
