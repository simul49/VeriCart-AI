# 9. User Personas

## 9.1 Introduction

User personas are fictional representations of real users based on expected behaviors, needs, motivations, and challenges.

Developing user personas allows the VeriCart AI development team to better understand who will use the platform, what problems they face, and how Artificial Intelligence can improve their shopping experience.

Rather than designing features based on assumptions, the system is designed around the needs of four primary stakeholders:

- Customer
- Seller
- Administrator
- AI Shopping Assistant

These personas guide product design, UI/UX decisions, system architecture, and AI functionality throughout the development process.

# 9.2 Primary Persona — Customer

## Persona Overview

| Attribute | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Name | Sarah Chen |
| Age | 27 |
| Occupation | Software Engineer |
| Education | Bachelor's Degree |
| Location | Shanghai, China |
| Shopping Frequency | 4–8 times per month |
| Devices | Smartphone & Laptop |
| Technical Knowledge | High |

---

## Background

Sarah frequently purchases electronics, books, fashion items, and household products online.

Before making a purchase, she usually compares multiple products and reads customer reviews.

However, she often finds that:

- Reviews contradict each other.
- Many reviews appear fake.
- It takes too much time to evaluate products.

She wants AI to simplify this process.

---

## Goals

- Buy reliable products.
- Save time reading reviews.
- Avoid fake products.
- Receive trustworthy recommendations.
- Compare products intelligently.
- Understand product quality before purchasing.

---

## Frustrations

- Too many reviews.
- Fake ratings.
- Sponsored reviews.
- Poor recommendations.
- Information overload.

---

## Needs

Sarah needs:

- AI Review Summary
- Trust Score
- Shopping Assistant
- Product Comparison
- Personalized Recommendations

---

## Motivations

Sarah values:

- Trust
- Transparency
- Convenience
- Time savings
- Reliable information

---

## User Story

> As a customer,

> I want AI to summarize customer reviews

> so that I can quickly understand whether a product is worth buying.

---

## Success Criteria

Sarah can:

- Find products quickly.
- Understand review summaries.
- View Trust Scores.
- Compare products.
- Complete purchases confidently.

# 9.5 AI Shopping Assistant Persona

Unlike traditional software systems, VeriCart AI includes an intelligent AI Shopping Assistant that actively supports customers throughout their purchasing journey.

---

## Persona Overview

| Attribute | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Name | Veri |
| Type | AI Shopping Assistant |
| Personality | Helpful, Transparent, Professional |
| Availability | 24/7 |
| Languages | English, Chinese (Future: Japanese, Korean) |

---

## Responsibilities

Veri helps customers by:

- Answering shopping questions.
- Comparing products.
- Explaining Trust Scores.
- Summarizing reviews.
- Providing recommendations.

---

## Example Questions

Customers may ask:

- Should I buy this product?
- Why is this Trust Score high?
- Which product has better reviews?
- What complaints do customers mention?

# 9.10 Accessibility Considerations

VeriCart AI is designed to provide an inclusive experience.

The platform should support:

- Responsive design
- Keyboard navigation
- Readable typography
- High-contrast interfaces
- Screen-reader compatibility
- Simple navigation
- Multilingual support

These considerations ensure that users with different technical abilities and accessibility needs can effectively use the platform.

# 9.11 Chapter Summary

The user personas provide a comprehensive understanding of the primary stakeholders interacting with VeriCart AI.

By identifying customer goals, frustrations, motivations, and behaviors, the development team can design features that directly address real-world problems.

The Customer remains the primary focus of the platform, while Sellers and Administrators benefit from AI-driven analytics and moderation tools. The AI Shopping Assistant acts as an intelligent companion that supports users throughout the purchasing journey.

These personas establish the foundation for the next chapter, **User Journey & User Stories**, where detailed interaction flows and functional requirements will be defined.
